Use FTS to locate promising evidence; then inspect a focused time context or activity range around it to test sequences, repetitions, and cross-application transitions. Use source lookup only when a single record needs more detail. Explore competing explanations before calling a pattern relevant; do not mistake one incidental record for a workflow.

Keep observed evidence distinct in your reasoning from user-provided context. Do not introduce a business domain, role, causal rule, or step that the packet and retrieved evidence do not support. Prefer the concrete names, artifacts, applications, and actions that were actually observed; leave interpretation and unknowns explicit.
